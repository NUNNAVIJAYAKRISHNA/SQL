Indexing and Hashing

For a dbms, consisting many rows will decrease performance because it scans each and every row for the requirement. That's why we have 2 concepts called Indexing and Hashing. 

These are only used for retreival of data.

B-Tree and B+ Tree

These two organize the data in form of trees so that it will be easier for data reads.
But even B tree checks all nodes

That's why B+ tree is there, it optimizes the tree by arranging the elements in an orderly manner.
